
# Ticket 001: Managed Device Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

**Environment:** SysDesks ticketing simulation  
**Category:** Mobile Devices > Wi-Fi  
**Tier:** Tier 1  
**Status:** Resolved

## 1. Reported Issue
The warehouse tablet could access public websites but could not connect to the company's Inventory Scan service.

## 2. Troubleshooting Steps
1. Connected to the simulated user's device through a remote session.
2. Checked the currently connected Wi-Fi network.
3. Identified that the device was connected to public Wi-Fi instead of corporate Wi-Fi.

## 3. Root Cause
The device was connected to the wrong wireless network, preventing access to the company service.

## 4. Resolution
Connected the device to the corporate Wi-Fi network while preserving its existing company management configuration.

## 5. Verification
Retried the Inventory Scan service and confirmed that connectivity was successful.

## 6. Skills Demonstrated
- Remote troubleshooting
- Wi-Fi and network connectivity
- Managed mobile device support
- Root cause identification
- Resolution verification

## 7. Lessons Learned
A device may have internet access while still being unable to reach internal company services. Verify network selection and access to the specific application before pursuing more disruptive troubleshooting steps.
